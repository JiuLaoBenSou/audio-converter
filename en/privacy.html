ZhuanMP3 Privacy Policy: local audio, ads and analytics

Your audio is not uploaded. Website visits, ads and external services have separate data practices.

Applies to zhuanmp3.com. Updated November 1, 2026. Maintainer: Foster Fei. Privacy contact: fanghong1994@gmail.com.

1. Audio files and local storage

Audio you select or drop is not uploaded to a server. Recovery and conversion run locally in your browser. The tool reads files into memory, creates results and offers browser downloads without overwriting originals. It does not fetch artwork from addresses embedded in files.

The page manages its queue and results in memory; clearing the queue or closing the page releases the tool's references. You control original files, downloads and browser download history. The language preference is stored in this browser's localStorage as audio-converter-language, without automatic expiry, until changed or cleared. There are no user accounts, audio-upload endpoints or cloud audio storage.

2. Cookies and Google AdSense

The site uses Google AdSense code for publisher ca-pub-4153958256787466. Google and other third-party vendors may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites, measure ads and prevent abuse. Depending on settings and region, requests may involve IP addresses, browser information and device identifiers. Conversion does not depend on advertising cookies.

Hosting and security services may also use necessary security cookies. Cookie types, lifetimes and use depend on providers, browser settings and region. We do not promise one fixed lifetime. Including ad code does not guarantee an ad will appear.

3. Your advertising and browser choices

Use Google Ads Settings to adjust or opt out of personalized advertising. Some other vendors offer choices through industry opt-out tools. Opting out of personalization does not remove all ads. You can inspect, restrict or delete cookies and site data in your browser, including the saved language preference. Blocking scripts may prevent ads or analytics from working.

4. Analytics, collected data and retention

Cloudflare distributes and protects the site. Edge request statistics can involve time, IP address, URL, user agent, response status, security events and traffic summaries for reliability, security and performance. They do not include the audio selected in this tool. We have not separately integrated Google Analytics.

Cloudflare also injects its Web Analytics script for page visits and loading performance. Its documentation describes no visitor tracking through cookies, localStorage or fingerprinting. Browser security policy, blockers and network conditions affect whether the script reports; not every visit is recorded completely.

5. Minimal task counters

We use a separate Cloudflare Worker and D1 database to count processing outcomes by extension category, interface language and UTC day, plus individual/ZIP download triggers and ZIP-generation failures. The application stores daily totals only. It does not store audio, filenames, paths, tags, artwork, exact sizes or durations, raw errors, visitor identifiers, IP addresses or user agents in the counter table. Cloudflare still processes connection and operational data to run and protect the service.

The live aggregate table retains the latest 90 UTC dates, with scheduled daily cleanup. Provider recovery copies, connection logs and sampled operational diagnostics have separate retention rules; this is not a guarantee of irreversible deletion from every provider copy after 90 days. Test counts are labeled separately. A download trigger does not prove a file was saved, and no support/payment clicks or receipts are counted.

No tracking cookies or identifiers are added for these counters. Collection is best-effort and never required for conversion or downloads. Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control suppresses these requests. You may also open the tool with task counters disabled for that visit. This choice does not disable existing ads or Cloudflare edge traffic statistics.

6. External services and contact information

GitHub Pages hosts the site and Cloudflare provides distribution and security. These services process connections needed to deliver pages. If you use Ko-fi or a payment QR code, information you provide to that service follows its policies. This site does not receive your full payment credentials. Providers may process data outside your region.

Email fanghong1994@gmail.com for site-related requests. Your email address, message and attachments are used to reply and handle the request through the mailbox service. Send only necessary information. There is no uniform automatic deletion schedule for correspondence: retention or deletion depends on continued need, disputes and applicable obligations, with the mailbox provider's own practices also applying. Direct requests about third-party-held records to the relevant provider. Changes to this policy are published here.
