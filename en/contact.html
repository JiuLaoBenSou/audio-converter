Contact ZhuanMP3 and report an audio conversion issue

Keep the original file and describe what happened. You do not need to pay for help.

Contact the creator

ZhuanMP3 is maintained by Foster Fei. For feedback, privacy requests or rights concerns, email fanghong1994@gmail.com. Click the address to compose a message, or copy it into your email app. There is no on-site contact form or guaranteed response time.

Reporting a conversion problem

Include the file extension, approximate size, operating system, browser version and the exact queue error. Say whether processing failed or the downloaded result would not play. Do not send passwords, keys, sensitive payment information or music files you are not authorized to share.

Privacy and rights requests

Provide the relevant page URL, your concern and enough information to assess the request. Selected audio is not uploaded, so the creator cannot retrieve it from a server. Contact the relevant provider for records held by Google, Cloudflare, Ko-fi or a payment service. See the Privacy Policy.

Voluntary support and payment questions

Ko-fi is an optional support link, not the contact channel you must pay to use. Chinese payment QR codes remain available in the Chinese tool. Support does not purchase download access, membership or a service commitment. Check the recipient and amount before paying; the website does not process card details or charges.

For a mistaken payment or refund request, email the channel, time and amount, and consult that payment platform's support options. Do not post full transaction identifiers or personal details publicly. Refund requests require verification and follow the payment channel's rules; there is no automatic or guaranteed refund.

Before reporting

Try a current browser and one file at a time. The per-file limit is 100 MiB; ordinary audio conversion supports up to 20 minutes. Unsupported versions requiring extra keys cannot be recovered here. Read the guide and limits.
