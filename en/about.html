About ZhuanMP3: local audio conversion and recovery

Make your own audio files easier to play in your preferred player.

What this tool does

ZhuanMP3 is an independent audio tool maintained by Foster Fei. Convert ordinary audio your browser can decode, such as OGG, FLAC, WAV and M4A, to 192 kbps MP3. You can also attempt to recover supported NCM, QMC and other special-format files. No account is required.

These are different operations. Recovery keeps the original audio encoding, so the result may be MP3, FLAC, OGG or another supported format. A recognized extension does not guarantee compatibility with every client version. Existing MP3 files are kept as-is after validation.

Your audio stays on your device

Selected audio is processed in your browser and is not uploaded to a server. The tool does not overwrite originals. Web hosting, advertisements and traffic statistics can still make network requests; see our Privacy Policy.

Free to use, optional to support

Conversion and downloads are free. The tool pages include Google AdSense code, and voluntary support helps maintain the site. You can support Foster on Ko-fi. Support never unlocks a download or guarantees a service. Ads may not appear on every visit.

Limits and responsible use

This project is not affiliated with NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music or other music platforms. It provides no song catalog, download sources, account access or copyright license. Only process files you own or are authorized to use, and follow the source platform's terms. Versions requiring extra keys are not supported. Keep your originals and test results in your target player.
