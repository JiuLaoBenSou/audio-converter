    Common questions

    Can I convert OGG, FLAC and WAV to MP3?

    Everyday audio that your browser can decode is converted to 192 kbps MP3, up to 20 minutes and 100 MB per file. Existing MP3 files are kept as-is. MP3 encoding is lossy and does not improve source quality.

    Will NCM and QMC always become MP3?

    No. Special-format recovery preserves the original audio, which may be MP3, FLAC or OGG. Versions requiring an additional key are not supported.

    Why do some files fail?

    A file may use an unsupported version, be damaged, or require decoding your browser does not support. Try a current browser. Renaming a file extension does not convert its contents.
